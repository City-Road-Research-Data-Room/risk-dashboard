Two-Tier Risk Architecture

City Road does not treat market making and alpha strategies as separate businesses. They share the same data spine, execution gateways, risk engine, capital allocation process, kill-switches, custody controls and governance framework.

Tier 1 · Market Making & Liquidity Provision

Strategies that seek to earn net spread, rebates, funding or liquidity incentives by quoting selected markets where risk-adjusted economics are attractive.

Primary risksInventory build-up, adverse selection, stale data, execution failure, latency, volatility shocks.
ControlsInventory caps, quote widening, liquidity pull, post-fill markouts, data freshness gates.
Scaling ruleScale only where spread / rebate / funding economics compensate for risk and slippage.

Tier 2 · Proprietary Alpha Strategies

Strategies that seek to capture mispricings from cross-venue price discovery, sports/event modelling, RWA dislocations, flow classification and research signals.

Primary risksModel error, overfitting, stale edge, directional exposure, event risk, signal decay, correlation.
ControlsOut-of-sample testing, shadow mode, micro-live validation, event caps, edge decay limits.
Scaling ruleNo material capital until live evidence proves data quality, execution quality and repeatable edge.

Strategy Deployment Pipeline

No model moves directly from backtest to meaningful capital deployment. Tier 1 and Tier 2 strategies must prove data quality, execution quality, controlled loss behaviour and repeatable edge.

1. Research / simulation

Historical analysis, out-of-sample testing, cost assumptions, hypothesis clarity.

2. Live shadow mode

Real market data, no capital. Records signals, quotes, expected fills and markouts.

3. Micro-live testing

Very small capital to test execution, latency, fills, settlement and controls.

4. Limited deployment

Strict strategy, venue and drawdown caps. Manual review after each cycle.

5. Scaled deployment

Capital increases only after performance, risk, cashflow recycling and operational controls are validated.
